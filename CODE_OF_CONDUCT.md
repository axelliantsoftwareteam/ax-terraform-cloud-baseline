# Code of Conduct

Contact: https://axelliant.com | info@axelliant.com | security@axelliant.com

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all discussions.
- Focus feedback on technical outcomes and collaboration.
- Assume good intent while holding a high engineering bar.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or interactions that violate this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to info@axelliant.com. Security-related concerns should be sent to security@axelliant.com.
